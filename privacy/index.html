Privacy Policy

Last updated October 13, 2026
This is a starting template written in plain English. It is not legal advice and should be reviewed by a lawyer before you rely on it.

Cert Compliance ("the app", "we") is operated by Jake Storm. This policy explains what information the app stores, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: jakestorm@cert-comp.com.

What we store

  • Account details: name, email address, role (Admin, Scheduler, Tech), position and department.
  • Contact details: phone numbers that a company adds for its people.
  • Certifications: which certs each person holds, acquired and expiry dates, and any photos of cert cards uploaded to the app.
  • Jobs and sites: job details, crews, customers, site addresses and coordinates, and site access information such as gate codes and access notes.
  • Activity: a log of changes (who changed what, and when) so companies can see recent activity.

How we use it

Only to run the app: signing you in, showing your company its crews, jobs and cert status, sending invite emails, and sending cert expiry reminders. We do not use your information for advertising.

Who can see it

Each company's information is kept separate. People in a company see only what their role allows. For example, gate codes and access notes are shown only to the people assigned to that job. The operator can access data when needed to support or maintain the service.

Your company owns its data

The information a company enters belongs to that company. Its Admins can download a copy at any time.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run the app, and they process data only for that purpose:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage (United States).
  • Resend: sending invite and reminder emails.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting the app's web pages.

We don't sell data

We never sell, rent or trade your information.

Backups and retention

Automatic backups are kept for about 30 days so data can be restored if something goes wrong. If a company's access is turned off, its data is kept unless the company asks us to delete it.

Deleting your data

A company can ask us to delete its data, and a person can ask to have their account removed, by emailing jakestorm@cert-comp.com. We will delete it within a reasonable time, including from backups as they expire.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and access is restricted by company and role. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, let company Admins know.
